About Us

Measured by results.

Who we are

Glenro is a leader in the provision of professional and comprehensive built environment and commercial services with specific focus on challenges in the engineering and built environment industry.

Glenro is your one stop shop for all your built environment and commercial services for any project. Our team of construction contract specialists, engineers and project managers will ensure that your next project is managed and executed from the procurement stage right through to final completion.

How we work

We start with the contract and the record. Every recommendation traces back to a clause, a certificate or a site document.

  • Contract specialists, engineers and project managers on one team.
  • Advice from procurement through to final completion.
  • Legal and engineering opinion produced together, not handed between firms.
