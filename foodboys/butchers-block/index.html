    Break down a carcass

    Choose a carcass, weigh each cut and post it. The carcass cost is split across the cuts by sales value, so fillet carries more of the cost per kg than mince.

    Talk it in

    Say or type it the way you would at the block, for example: "carcass of 330 kg beef, 20 kg loin, 20 kg rib, 15 kg rump". The weights fill in below for you to check. Cuts you do not mention stay at 0, and nothing is posted until you tap Post breakdown. On a phone you can use the microphone on your keyboard.

    Import a till report

    Paste the end-of-day sales export from the till or scale, one line per cut with the mass in kg. Each line is matched to a cut and you check it before recording.

    Stock take

    Weigh what is in the display and cold room. Any difference is posted to stock losses, so drip, trim and waste show up in the books.

    Journals for the accountant

    Ready-made double entries for the period. Rename the accounts to match your chart of accounts in Pastel, Xero or Sage.

    Cuts & yields

    Set your selling prices and the expected yield of each cut from a carcass. Update the yields after your first few cutting tests.
